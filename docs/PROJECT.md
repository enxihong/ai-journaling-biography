## Judging criteria (1–7 scale each, weighted)

| Criterion | Weight | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| Meaningful Use of AI | 25% | Not just "call an LLM" — extraction, pattern detection, source-grounded generation. Biggest single weight, prioritize this. |
| Problem & Idea Clarity | 20% | Pitch must state the problem in one sentence + who it's for. Practice the 3-min pitch. |
| Execution & Product | 20% | A working demo of the CORE LOOP beats many half-built features. |
| Creativity & Originality | 20% | Family Memory Graph / unclaimed profiles / legacy framing are our differentiators — mention them even if only partly built. |
| Collaboration & Communication | 15% | How well the team explains "what we built and why" — assign this to the pitch/storytelling role explicitly. |

**Judging philosophy (their words):** not looking for a unicorn or a flawless product — genuine engagement with AI, real progress, ability to clearly explain what was built and why. A simple, well-understood project scores as well as an ambitious one.
